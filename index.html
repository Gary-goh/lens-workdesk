Lens Conversion Workdesk

Offline mobile version. Enter spectacle prescription details to estimate contact-lens category, vertex-adjusted power, and whether the case is likely stock range or MTO.

Important Notes
1. This tool is for sales support and training, not a final clinical fitting decision.
2. Final lens selection still depends on K readings, corneal topography, tear film, wearing goals, and practitioner judgment.
3. Brand limits differ. A case marked possible may still become MTO depending on diameter, base curve, axis availability, and supplier rules.
